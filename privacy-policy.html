01 Information We Access and How We Use It

FocusFlight operates predominantly offline. We only request the minimum device capabilities necessary to deliver the core flight simulator features:

Geographic Location (Precise & Coarse)

When granted, location is queried strictly on-demand to find your nearest real-world departure airport and show your local city on your boarding pass and radar. Coordinates are processed locally through Android’s Geocoder and are never stored on or sent to remote developer servers. If location permission is declined, FocusFlight gracefully defaults to a curated route catalog.

  • Flight Records & Statistics: Tracked focus durations, destination airports, focus tags (e.g. Deep Work, Study), and earned miles are saved locally in the device's Room database.
  • App Preferences: Selected cabin soundscapes, volume settings, and map style selections are saved in local private SharedPreferences.
02 Device Permissions

Google Play requires clear disclosure of all sensitive device permissions used by the application:

Permission Type Justification / In-App Purpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 Location Determines the nearest airport for your origin departure point and centers the flight radar.
ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Do Not Disturb Powers "Airplane Mode", muting notification interruptions upon takeoff and restoring previous settings upon landing.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Presents a live countdown notification during flights and alerts you upon arrival at your destination.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
SPECIAL_USE / MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Background Service Keeps your focus countdown running and streams ambient cabin audio reliably while the screen is off.
INTERNET Network Loads vector map tiles and queries real-time public airspace flight positions.
03 Third-Party Services

When loading map visuals or displaying active air traffic, FocusFlight makes requests to reputable public and open-source data services. These requests transmit standard network metadata (such as device IP address):

  • Map Styles & Cartography: CARTO Basemaps and OpenStreetMap (CARTO Privacy, OSM Foundation).
  • Satellite Tiles: NASA EOSDIS Global Imagery Browse Services (NASA Privacy).
  • Real-time Airspace Data: OpenSky Network API (OpenSky Network Terms).
  • Location Providers: Google Play Services Location (Google Privacy Policy).
04 User Data Deletion & Retention

Because FocusFlight stores all flight and session data entirely on your device, you have immediate and complete control over your data deletion at all times.

1
Open Settings on your Android device.
2
Navigate to Apps → FocusFlight → Storage & Cache.
3
Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data to permanently wipe all logs and statistics.

* Note: Uninstalling FocusFlight automatically deletes all local app files and databases.
